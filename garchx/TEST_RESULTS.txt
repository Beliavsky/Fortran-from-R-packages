Notes
-----
GNU ld reports that garchx_model.o requests an executable stack. This is caused
by GNU Fortran trampolines for internal procedure callbacks passed to the
optimizer and numerical-Hessian routines. The binaries executed successfully
in both validation configurations. Exact R optimizer and random-stream
reproduction is not claimed. R and fpm were unavailable in the validation
environment.
