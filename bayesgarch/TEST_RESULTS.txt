What the tests establish
------------------------

- Exact hard-coded checks for the GARCH(1,1), general GARCH, threshold-GARCH,
  alpha-filter, asymmetric alpha-filter, W-filter, asymmetric W-filter, and
  quasi-difference recursions.
- Exact hard-coded checks for the standardized Student-t likelihood,
  augmented log posterior, one- and two-parameter proposal regressions,
  degrees-of-freedom root equation, and acceptance probability.
- Simulation/filter identities for GARCH and threshold-GARCH routines.
- Fixed-seed moment checks for Normal, Gamma, Exponential, and standardized
  Student-t random generation, plus digamma reference values.
- Fixed-seed multi-chain sampler checks for positive parameters, nu > 2,
  movement of all sampling blocks, covariance stationarity, a user-supplied
  prior constraint, posterior burn-in/thinning, and chain concatenation.

Limitations of validation
-------------------------

R was not installed in the validation environment. Complete random chains were
therefore not compared draw-for-draw with the R package. The Fortran project
uses its own random-number generators, so bit-for-bit chain equivalence is not
expected. No full statistical-equivalence claim is made.
