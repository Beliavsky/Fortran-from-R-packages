## DEoptim

The optimization engine embedded in `src/deoptim_*.f90` and `src/deoptim.f90`
comes from the user-supplied `DEoptim-fortran` 0.1.0 translation of R package
DEoptim 2.2-8. That translation identifies the original DEoptim package as
GPL (>= 2). Its original/translated sources, license files, attribution, and
translation notes are retained verbatim under:

`vendor/DEoptim-fortran-v0.1.0/`

GPL-2-or-later code is compatible with distribution of this combined work under
GPL-3.
