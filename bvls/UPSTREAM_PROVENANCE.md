# Upstream provenance

Source archive: `bvls` 1.4.

The package describes itself as an R interface to the Stark-Parker
implementation accompanying:

P. B. Stark and R. L. Parker (1995), *Bounded-Variable Least-Squares: An
Algorithm and Applications*, Computational Statistics 10, 129-141.

The upstream `inst/COPYRIGHTS` states that the source was obtained from the
Statlib BVLS distribution and that the authors agreed to distribution under
GPL version 2 or newer.

The complete supplied source tree is preserved verbatim under
`original/bvls-master/`.
