The original package and translated source are distributed under the BSD
2-Clause License. The original package source is included under `original/` for
license and provenance traceability.

Algorithm references retained from the package documentation:

- Schraudolph, Yu, and Guenter (2007), online L-BFGS.
- Byrd, Hansen, Nocedal, and Singer (2016), stochastic quasi-Newton.
- Keskar and Berahas (2016), adaQN.
- Nocedal and Wright, Numerical Optimization, limited-memory BFGS recursion.
