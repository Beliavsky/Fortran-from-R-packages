# Notice

This is a modern Fortran translation of the computational algorithms in FRAPO
0.4-2, originally authored by Bernhard Pfaff.

Original citation:

Bernhard Pfaff (2016), *Financial Risk Modelling and Portfolio Optimisation
with R*, second edition, John Wiley & Sons Ltd, London.

The original package source, documentation, book examples, and data are retained
