## ZeroOneDists translation

- Year: 2025

The translated source under `src/`, tests, and examples are distributed under
the same MIT license. The complete MIT text is in `LICENSE`.

The original package snapshot is retained under `upstream/ZeroOneDists-1.0.0/`
for attribution and auditability.

## Supplied dependency translations

The supplied `gamlss-fortran-v0.9.0` and `gamlss.dist-fortran-v0.3.0` archives
both declare `GPL-3.0-only` in their FPM manifests. They are not copied or
linked into this package, so they do not change the license of the active MIT
library. They were used only as compatibility references during translation.
