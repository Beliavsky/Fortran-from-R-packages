# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial modern Fortran/FPM computational translation of GPareto 1.1.9.

- Exact Pareto/hypervolume and analytical bi-objective EHI kernels.
- SAA EHI/qEHI/EMI and SMS criteria.
- Direct posterior-conditioning SUR criterion.
- 2D/3D probability of non-domination.
- CPF/Vorob'ev and Pareto-set density workflows.
- DiceKriging-backed GP fitting, prediction, update, and re-estimation.
- Sequential optimization, target design search, integration designs and
  benchmark functions.
