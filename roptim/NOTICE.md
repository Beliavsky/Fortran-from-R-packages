## L-BFGS-B

L-BFGS-B 3.0 numerical kernel by Ciyou Zhu, Richard Byrd, Jorge Nocedal,
and the later 3.0 revision associated with Jorge Nocedal and Jose Luis
Morales. The numerical kernel is distributed under its stated New/Modified
BSD (3-clause) terms. The license text supplied with the source distribution
is retained in `LICENSES/LBFGSB-BSD-3-Clause.txt`.

The L-BFGS-B kernel is not relicensed under the GPL. The surrounding roptim
Fortran interface and the other solver implementations are GPL-2-or-later.
