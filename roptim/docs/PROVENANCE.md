is retained unchanged under `original_source/roptim-master` for attribution,
license traceability, and comparison.

- `src/roptim.h`
- `src/functor.h`
- `src/samin.h`
- `src/applic.h`
- `src/roptim_examples.cpp`
- `tests/testthat/test-roptim.R`
