# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial modern Fortran/FPM translation of mnormt 2.1.2.
- Normal and Student-t d/p/r functionality.
- Specialized and adaptive multivariate rectangle probabilities.
- Truncated normal/t density and CDF functionality.
- Native truncated-normal Gibbs sampler.
- Arbitrary-order truncated-normal moment recursion and cumulants through order 4.
- Mardia sample measures and positive-definite solver.
