## Numerical Linear Algebra

- [`Matrix`](../Matrix/) - Provides dense and sparse matrix algorithms.
- [`MASS`](../MASS/) - Provides statistical methods from Venables and Ripley's MASS.
- [`expm`](../expm/) - Computes matrix exponentials, logarithms, square roots, and related functions.
- [`irlba`](../irlba/) - Computes fast truncated singular-value decompositions.
- [`RSpectra`](../RSpectra/) - Computes selected eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and singular values of large matrices.

## Special Functions

- [`gsl`](../gsl/) - Wraps GNU GSL special functions and numerical routines.
- [`pracma`](../pracma/) - Provides practical numerical mathematics functions.
- [`hypergeo`](../hypergeo/) - Computes the Gauss hypergeometric function.
- [`elliptic`](../elliptic/) - Computes Weierstrass and Jacobi elliptic functions.
- [`expint`](../expint/) - Computes exponential integrals and incomplete gamma functions.

## Polynomials

- [`polynom`](../polynom/) - Provides arithmetic and calculations for univariate polynomials.
- [`orthopolynom`](../orthopolynom/) - Provides orthogonal and orthonormal polynomial families.

## Differentiation and Integration

- [`numDeriv`](../numDeriv/) - Computes accurate numerical gradients, Jacobians, and Hessians.
- [`cubature`](../cubature/) - Performs adaptive multivariate integration over hypercubes.
- [`statmod`](../statmod/) - Provides statistical-modeling utilities including inverse-Gaussian methods, quadrature, REML, and randomized quantile residuals.

## Root Finding and Fixed Points

- [`rootSolve`](../rootSolve/) - Finds nonlinear roots, equilibria, and steady states of ordinary differential equations.
- [`BB`](../BB/) - Solves and optimizes large-scale nonlinear systems.
- [`nleqslv`](../nleqslv/) - Solves systems of nonlinear equations.

## Discrete Mathematics and Number Theory

- [`contfrac`](../contfrac/) - Evaluates and manipulates continued fractions.
- [`magic`](../magic/) - Creates and analyzes magic squares, hypercubes, and Latin squares.
- [`partitions`](../partitions/) - Generates and analyzes additive integer partitions.

## MATLAB Octave Julia and other Interfaces

- [`matlab`](../matlab/) - Provides numerical and matrix utilities modelled after MATLAB functions.
