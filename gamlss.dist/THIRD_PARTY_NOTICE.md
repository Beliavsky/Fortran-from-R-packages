## gamlss.dist

This project is a computational translation of `gamlss.dist` 6.1-1. The
upstream DESCRIPTION identifies the package license as `GPL-2 | GPL-3` and
credits Mikis Stasinopoulos, Robert Rigby, and the listed contributors.
Unmodified package metadata, compiled reference sources, and selected R sources
used to check v0.2/v0.3 algorithms are retained under `upstream/`.

## Numerical support adapted from VGAM Fortran translation

Portable special-function, optimization, Student-t, base-distribution, random,
and linear-algebra support was adapted from the previously produced GPL-3 VGAM
Fortran computational translation. Source files retain applicable copyright and
SPDX notices, including T. W. Yee/University of Auckland notices where present.

Because the combined work includes GPL-3.0-only material, the Fortran package
as a whole is GPL-3.0-only. Copies of GPL-2 and GPL-3 are under `LICENSES/`;
`COPYING` contains GPL-3.
