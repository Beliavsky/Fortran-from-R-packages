# Notices

This package is a modern Fortran translation of computational routines from
MASS 7.3-66 by Brian Ripley, Bill Venables, and contributors. MASS is offered
under GPL version 2 or GPL version 3. The combined Fortran work is distributed
under GPL-3.0-only.

The `src/vendor/rrcov_*.f90` files originate from the previously completed
`rrcov-fortran-v0.1.0` translation and retain their GPL-3.0-or-later SPDX
headers. They provide self-contained linear algebra, robust covariance, and
discriminant-analysis foundations. A copy of that translation archive is kept
under `upstream/dependencies/` for provenance.

The original MASS archive is preserved unchanged under `upstream/`.
