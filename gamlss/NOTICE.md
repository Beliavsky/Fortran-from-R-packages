# Notices and provenance

This package is a modern Fortran computational translation of the R package
`gamlss` by Mikis Stasinopoulos, Robert Rigby and contributors.  The upstream
`DESCRIPTION`, `NAMESPACE`, `NEWS.md`, README, selected R computational sources,
and compiled C sources are retained under `upstream/`.

Upstream gamlss declares `GPL-2 | GPL-3`.  The combined Fortran distribution
backend used here is GPL-3.0-only, so this aggregate release is distributed
under GPL-3.0-only.  GPL-2 and GPL-3 license texts are retained under
`LICENSES/` and the individual vendored dependencies retain their own license
and notice files.

The supplied dependency ports are derivative works of their respective upstream
R packages; see the license/notice files inside each vendor directory.
