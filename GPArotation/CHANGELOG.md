# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial modern Fortran/FPM translation of GPArotation 2026.8-2.
- Orthogonal and oblique gradient-projection engines with BB, legacy and
  orthogonal Cayley step choices.
- Full upstream `vgQ` criterion set and numerical rotation wrapper surface.
- Random starts, Kaiser/Cureton-Mulaik normalization and Lp IRWLS rotation.
- EIV and echelon identification rotations.
- Residual, fit-statistic and simple-structure diagnostics.
- Finite-difference gradient tests and end-to-end rotation tests.
