# Validation

The permanent tests are:

- `test_distribution`: independent PMF/CDF reference values, log values,
  Poisson limits, and normalization
- `test_quantile`: independent quantiles, inversion inequalities, upper tails,
  and log probabilities
- `test_random`: seeded empirical mean and variance
- `test_saddlepoint`: PMF and CDF approximation errors over several quantiles
- `test_mle`: seeded recovery of two known rates and covariance diagnostics
- `test_regression`: seeded recovery of two log-link coefficient vectors and
  Hessian-based inference

The reference PMF, CDF, and quantile values in the tests are independent
numerical constants rather than values generated by the Fortran routines.

The release procedure builds and runs all tests, all five examples, and the
demo in both checked and optimized modes. It then repeats the process from a
clean extraction of the generated ZIP archive.
