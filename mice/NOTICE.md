algorithms from the R package **mice 3.19.0**, *Multivariate Imputation by
Chained Equations*.

as authors and Gerko Vink, Rianne Schouten, Alexander Robitzsch, Patrick
Rockenschaub, Lisa Doove, Shahab Jolani, Margarita Moreno-Betancur, Ian White,

Mingyang Cai, Thom Volker, Edoardo Costantini, Caspar van Lissa, Hanne Oberman,
Stephen Wade, Florian van Leeuwen, and Frederik Fabricius-Bjerre as
